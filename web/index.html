🛡️ Citizen Flood Survival Directives

Official directives from Ministry of Home Affairs (MoHA) & Nepal Red Cross Society.
💧

Drinking Water Safety

Never drink unboiled flood water. Use chlorine or Piyush drops (3 drops per liter), or boil water vigorously for 3+ minutes. Mix ORS (Jeevan Jal) with clean water to prevent diarrheal dehydration.

⚠️

Road & Causeway Hazards

Do not walk or drive through moving floodwaters. Just 6 inches of fast-moving water can knock down an adult, and 12 inches can sweep away small vehicles. Avoid submerged bridges and culverts.

🐍

Snakebite Precautions

Rising floodwaters force venomous snakes onto dry high-ground and trees. Always carry a bright flashlight and poke with a stick before stepping. In case of a bite, immobilize the limb and reach an operational clinic immediately.

🎒

Evacuation Go-Bag

Pack citizenship certificates, land deeds, and money in waterproof ziplock bags. Keep a flashlight, spare batteries, whistle, matchbox in plastic, dry beaten rice (chiura), and essential chronic medicines ready.
